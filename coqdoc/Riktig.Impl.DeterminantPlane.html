Determinants in the plane over the rationals, through the plane algebra's frame: of a pair of vectors, and of a 2 x 2 matrix.
Instance Plane_Det : HasDet Q bool V2 :=

Instance Plane_MatrixDeterminant : MatrixDeterminant Q bool Plane_MatDet :=
  Columns_MatrixDeterminant (V := V2) (N := nat) (basis := plane_basis) (DT := Plane_Det).
