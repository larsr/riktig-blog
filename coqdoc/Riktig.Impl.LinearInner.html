From Riktig Require Export Structure.InnerProductSpace Structure.Linear Impl.VectorSpaceField.

The inner product against a fixed vector is a linear map V → F, with F a vector space over itself.
Section LinearInner.
  Context {F V : Type} `{InnerProductSpace F V}.

  #[export] Instance Linear_Inner (v : V) : Linear F V F (fun u ⇒ ⟪ u , v ⟫) :=
    {| proper := fun u u' Hu ⇒ inner_proper u u' Hu v v (reflexivity v);
       is_additive := inner_additive v;
       is_homogeneous := inner_homogeneous v |}.
End LinearInner.
