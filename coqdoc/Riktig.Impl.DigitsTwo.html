From Riktig.Structure Require Export Digits.
From Riktig.Impl Require Export NatOrderNat.
From Riktig.Lib Require Export Coq.Two.

The two-element type as the digits of base two over Coq's naturals, its own carries: the sum is the parity and the carry the majority. Every cited fact is Lib.Coq.Two's.
Instance Two_HasRadix : HasRadix two nat := {| radix := 2 |}.
Instance Two_HasVal : HasVal two nat := two_index.
Instance Two_HasAddDigit : HasAddDigit two two := two_sum.
Instance Two_HasCarry : HasCarry two two := two_carry.

Instance Two_Digits : Digits two two nat Two_HasRadix Two_HasVal Two_HasVal Two_HasAddDigit Two_HasCarry (eqD := eq) (eqB := eq) := {|
  Digits.equivalence_digit := eq_equivalence;
  Digits.equivalence_carry := eq_equivalence;
  Digits.val_digit_proper := fun b b' E ⇒ f_equal two_index E;
  Digits.val_carry_proper := fun b b' E ⇒ f_equal two_index E;
  Digits.add_digit_proper := fun x x' Ex y y' Ey c c' Ec ⇒ f_equal3 two_sum Ex Ey Ec;
  Digits.carry_proper := fun x x' Ex y y' Ey c c' Ec ⇒ f_equal3 two_carry Ex Ey Ec;
  Digits.val_digit_lt := two_index_lt_two;
  Digits.val_carry_lt := two_index_lt_two;
  Digits.add_digit_spec := two_adder_spec;
|}.
