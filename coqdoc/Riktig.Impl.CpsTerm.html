From Coq Require Import String List.
From Riktig Require Export Structure.CPSLambda Impl.LambdaTerm Lib.Coq.Cps.

Fischer's call-by-value translation puts the de Bruijn terms in continuation-passing style, and is correct for every terminating call-by-value run.
Definition evaluates (t v : term) : Prop := cbvs t v ∧ val v = true.

Instance Term_CPSLambda : CPSLambda nat term sub triv tail cps psi evaluates.
Proof.
  split.
  - exact triv_var.
  - exact triv_lam.
  - exact tail_triv.
  - exact tail_call.
  - exact tail_call2.
  - exact cps_trivial.
  - intros v k Hv. exact (cps_value v k Hv).
  - intros l v k [Hr Hv]. exact (cps_correct l v Hr Hv k).
Qed.
