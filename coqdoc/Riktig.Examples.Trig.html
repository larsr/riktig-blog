Over any trigonometry: the point (x, y) turned by the angle t.
Section OverAnyTrig.
  Context {T : Type} `{Trig T}.

  Definition rotate (t x y : T) : T × T :=
    (cos t × x + - (sin t × y), sin t × x + cos t × y).
End OverAnyTrig.

At Coq's reals the theorems apply as they stand.
Example pythagoras_at_R : ∀ t : R, cos t × cos t + sin t × sin t == 1.
Proof. exact cos_sq_add_sin_sq. Qed.

Example rotate_at_R : ∀ t : R, rotate t 1 0 == (cos t, sin t).
Proof. intros t. split; [ exact (rotate_unit_fst t) | exact (rotate_unit_snd t) ]. Qed.
