From Coq Require Import Reals.
From Riktig Require Import Theory.Trig Impl.TrigR.
Local Close Scope R_scope.

Over any trigonometry: the point (x, y) turned by the angle t.
Section OverAnyTrig.
  Context {T : Type} `{Trig T}.

  Definition rotate (t x y : T) : T × T :=
    (cos t × x + - (sin t × y), sin t × x + cos t × y).
End OverAnyTrig.

At Coq's reals the theorems apply as they stand.
Example pythagoras_at_R : ∀ t : R, (cos t × cos t + sin t × sin t = 1)%R.
Proof. exact cos_sq_add_sin_sq. Qed.

Example rotate_at_R : ∀ t : R, rotate t 1%R 0%R = (cos t, sin t).
Proof.
  intros t. unfold rotate.
  cbv [add mult neg zero one R_HasAdd R_HasMult R_HasNeg R_Zero R_One].
  f_equal; ring.
Qed.
