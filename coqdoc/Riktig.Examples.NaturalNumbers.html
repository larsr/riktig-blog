Coq's nat is the natural numbers of Coq's types -- O and S are the two halves of one build -- and from that alone it is a Naturals: the arithmetic is folds, and the laws are initiality.
Compute (7 + 8 : nat).
Compute (7 × 8 : nat).

Example commutes (a b : nat) : a + b == b + a := add_comm a b.
