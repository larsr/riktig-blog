The de Bruijn CPS translation, defunctionalized: continuations are the stacks dcont read back by reify, and a Compiler.machine run is a CK run on source terms.
Instance Term_Defunctionalization : Defunctionalization nat term sub dcont
    (fun t : term ⇒ val t = true) Halt KArg KFun reify druns.
Proof.
  split.
  - intros b. reflexivity.
  - intros v Hv. split; [ apply star_refl | exact Hv ].
  - exact reify_halt_joinable.
  - exact reify_karg_joinable.
  - intros a f k _. exact (reify_kfun_joinable a f k).
  - intros b v k Hv. exact (reify_call_joinable b v k Hv).
  - intros t v k Hd. exact (druns_cps t v k Hd).
  - intros l v [Hr Hv]. exact (cbvs_druns l v Hr Hv).
Qed.
