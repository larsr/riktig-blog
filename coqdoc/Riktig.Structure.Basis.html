Library Riktig.Structure.Basis

From Riktig.Structure Require Export VectorSpace Finite NatRec Decidable.

A basis of a vector space, indexed by a finite type with decided equality: a family of vectors and a coordinate map, both respecting equality, the coordinates linear in the vector, every vector the combination of the basis with its coordinates, and the coordinates of a basis vector the unit at its index.
Module Basis.
  Class Basis (F I V N : Type) `{VectorSpace F V} `{Finite I N} {EI : EquDec I}
      {natrecN : HasNatRec N} {NR : NatRec N natrecN}
      (basis : I → V) (coordinates : HasCoords F I V) := {
    basis_proper :: Proper (equ ==> equ) basis;
    coords_proper :: Proper (equ ==> equ ==> equ) (@coords F I V coordinates);
    coords_additive : ∀ i : I, Additive V F add add (fun v ⇒ coords v i);
    coords_homogeneous : ∀ i : I, Homogeneous F V F mult (fun a ⇒ a) (fun v ⇒ coords v i);
    reconstruction : ∀ v : V, natrec V 0 (fun k acc ⇒ acc + coords v (elem k) *: basis (elem k)) card == v;
    coords_basis : ∀ i j : I, coords (basis j) i == (if equ_dec i j then (1 : F) else 0);
  }.
  Hint Mode Basis - ! ! - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - : typeclass_instances.
End Basis.
