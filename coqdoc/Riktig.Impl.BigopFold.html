From Riktig Require Export Structure.BigOp.

Instance BigOp_fold {M : Type} {Me : EqRel M} {Mz : Zero M} {Ma : HasAdd M}
    {MR : Reflexive (@equ M Me)} : BigOp M (@bigop_list M _ _).
Proof.
  split; intros; reflexivity.
Qed.

bigopI f is the fold of f over a finite index carrier's enumeration.
Notation bigopI f := (bigop_list f enum).

bigop n f is that fold over the first n naturals.
Notation bigop n f := (bigop_list f (seq 0 n)).
