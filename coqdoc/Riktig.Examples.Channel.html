A channel between two composed programs disappears by one rewrite; stacked channels disappear one law at a time.
Section Rewiring.
  Context {O : Type} `{Category O} {x A W V y : O}
    (f : x ~> A) (g : A ~> y)
    (e1 : A ~> W) (d1 : W ~> A) `{!Channel O A W e1 d1}
    (e2 : W ~> V) (d2 : V ~> W) `{!Channel O W V e2 d2}.

  Example rewired : (g ∘ d1) ∘ (e1 ∘ f) == g ∘ f :=
    through_channel e1 d1 x y f g.

Instance resolution chains the two channels by itself.
  Example rewired_twice : (g ∘ (d1 ∘ d2)) ∘ ((e2 ∘ e1) ∘ f) == g ∘ f :=
    through_channel (e2 ∘ e1) (d1 ∘ d2) x y f g.
End Rewiring.
