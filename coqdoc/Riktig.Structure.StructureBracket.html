A table of structure constants makes a Lie algebra of coordinate vectors. The bracket is the double sum the table weights, and all three Lie laws come from two facts about the table alone: that it changes sign when its first two indices are swapped, and that the Jacobi sum vanishes on the standard basis. Nothing in this file knows how many generators there are or what the numbers say — su(3)'s own table, its nine numbers and its eight generators, is Impl/SU3.v, which checks the second fact and instantiates the theorem at the end.

Module StructureBracket.
The equations that make x , y the bracket of the table f: the value at output coordinate c is the double sum weighted by the table, and the table changes sign under a swap of its first two indices. A class of equations rather than a definition, so that the computing table lives in Impl and Theory reasons from the equations alone — the arrangement Theory/Fin.v's Swap has with Impl/Fin.v's swap.
The sum is a class PARAMETER carrying its two laws, for the reason Structure/BigOp.v gives and Structure/MatMul.v and Structure/Lincomb.v are also written on: the value at c is a double sum over the generators, and a law naming one particular sum is a law no other sum can satisfy. Until 2026-08-18 this field said bigopF, Impl/FinIndex.v's structural fold over Fin.t, which is why this was the one Structure file that required an Impl one. Which sum a bracket is a double sum of is now chosen by the instance: Impl/SU3.v's picks the fold, and Theory/SU3Algebra.v names it once and bridges once.
