A field is an inner product space over itself: the inner product is multiplication, and the laws are distributivity, associativity and commutativity read at it.
Section FieldInner.
  Context {F : Type} `{Field F}.

  #[export] Instance Field_Inner : HasInner F F := mult.

  #[export] Instance Field_InnerProductSpace : InnerProductSpace F F :=
    {| InnerProductSpace.inner_proper := op_proper;
       InnerProductSpace.inner_additive := fun v u u' ⇒ right_distributive u u' v;
       InnerProductSpace.inner_homogeneous := fun v a u ⇒ associative a u v;
       InnerProductSpace.is_commutative := is_mul_commutative |}.
End FieldInner.
