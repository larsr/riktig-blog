Terms are written as on paper, and the abstract theorems apply to them directly.
Example identity_beta : ∀ t : term, ⦅(λ #0) t⦆ == t.
Proof. exact beta_id. Qed.

Church's conditional picks its branch -- proven once, for every lambda calculus.
Example if_picks_true : ∀ g h : term, church_if church_true g h == g.
Proof. exact church_if_true. Qed.

Arithmetic up to conversion: normalize both sides and compare.
Local Notation TWO := (λ λ ⦅#1 ⦅#1 #0⦆⦆).
Local Notation THREE := (λ λ ⦅#1 ⦅#1 ⦅#1 #0⦆⦆⦆).
Local Notation FIVE := (λ λ ⦅#1 ⦅#1 ⦅#1 ⦅#1 ⦅#1 #0⦆⦆⦆⦆⦆).
Local Notation PLUS := (λ λ λ λ ⦅#3 #1 ⦅#2 #1 #0⦆⦆).

Example add_two_three : ⦅PLUS TWO THREE⦆ == FIVE.
Proof. apply (joinable_of_normalize 5 0). vm_compute. reflexivity. Qed.

Distinct variables are distinct terms: the conversion setoid is not degenerate, by confluence.
Example var_zero_not_one : #0 =/= #1.
Proof. apply var_discrete. discriminate. Qed.
