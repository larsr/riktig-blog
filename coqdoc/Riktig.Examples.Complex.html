Over any complex numbers: the squared modulus, and the inverse of a nonzero number as its conjugate over its squared modulus.
Section OverAnyComplex.
  Context {F C : Type} `{Complex F C}.

  Definition modulus_sq (z : C) : F := re z × re z + im z × im z.

  Definition inverse (z : C) : C := inv (modulus_sq z) *: conj z.
End OverAnyComplex.

At the rationals doubled at - 1: (1 + 2 i) (3 + 4 i) = - 5 + 10 i, by evaluation and by the product formula, and the inverse of 3 + 4 i is (3 - 4 i) / 25.
Notation C := (Double (- (1 : Q)) Q).
Definition z : C := (1, 2)%Q.
Definition w : C := (3, 4)%Q.

Example zw_eval : z × w == (- 5, 10)%Q.
Proof. vm_compute. split; reflexivity. Qed.

Example re_zw : re (z × w) == re z × re w + - (im z × im w).
Proof. exact (re_mul z w). Qed.

Eval vm_compute in modulus_sq w.
Eval vm_compute in inverse w.

Example inverse_w : w × inverse w == 1.
Proof. vm_compute. split; reflexivity. Qed.
