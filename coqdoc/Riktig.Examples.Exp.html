The number e at Coq's Cauchy reals: the limit of the partial sums of the exponential series at 1, a Cauchy sequence by the comparison test, whose modulus the theory computes.
Definition e : CReal :=
  lim (N := nat) _ (exp_summable (R := CReal) (Q := Q) (N := nat) (of_nat := Q_of_nat) (inject_Q 1%Q)).

Eval vm_compute in (approx e 0.001)%Q.

e is 2.718 to within a thousandth, and the approximation is checked by evaluation.
Example e_approx : (Qabs (approx e 0.001 - 2.718) ≤ 0.002)%Q.
Proof. vm_compute. intro H. discriminate H. Qed.

The sine at one half, by its series: the terms shrink by the ratio test, the partial sums are Cauchy, and the limit approximates to 0.479....
Definition sin_half : CReal :=
  lim (N := nat) _ (sin_summable (R := CReal) (Q := Q) (N := nat) (of_nat := Q_of_nat) (inject_Q 0.5)).

Eval vm_compute in (approx sin_half 0.001)%Q.

Example sin_half_approx : (Qabs (approx sin_half 0.001 - 0.479) ≤ 0.002)%Q.
Proof. vm_compute. intro H0. discriminate H0. Qed.
