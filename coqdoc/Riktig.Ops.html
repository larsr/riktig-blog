The lambda calculus: variables by index, abstraction, application, and explicit substitutions t.[s] built from the identity, the shift, consing a term (u .: s) and lifting under a binder. A datatype is described by a polynomial signature over a set K of constant names: 0 no constructors, 1 a constructor with no fields, self a recursive occurrence, const k a field named by k, + a choice of constructor and × a field beside the rest. Zero, one, plus and times are the classes above; the recursor answers one value for each of the six operations.
