Library Riktig.Structure.MinusOneNotASquare

From Riktig.Structure Require Export Field.

A field in which a sum of two squares vanishes only trivially — equivalently, - (1) is not a square, which is exactly the condition for F[i] to be a field (Impl/Complex.v). Strictly weaker than formal reality, which forbids vanishing sums of squares of every length.
Module MinusOneNotASquare.
  Class MinusOneNotASquare (F : Type) `{Field F} :=
    two_squares_vanish : ∀ a b : F, a × a + b × b == 0 → a == 0.
  Hint Mode MinusOneNotASquare ! - - - - - - - - : typeclass_instances.
End MinusOneNotASquare.
