From Riktig Require Export Structure.Table.

Functions I → A are tables over I, equal when they agree at every index; reading is application and tabulating is the identity. The index is compared by Leibniz equality.
Section TableFun.
  Context {I A : Type} {eqA : EqRel A} {EA : Equivalence eqA}.

  #[export] Instance Fun_EqRel : EqRel (I → A) := pointwise_relation I equ.
  #[export] Instance Fun_HasGet : HasGet (I → A) I A := fun f i ⇒ f i.
  #[export] Instance Fun_HasTabulate : HasTabulate (I → A) I A := fun f ⇒ f.

  #[export] Instance Fun_Table : Table I A (I → A) Fun_HasGet Fun_HasTabulate (eqI := eq).
  Proof.
    split.
    - split.
      + intros f i. reflexivity.
      + intros f g Hfg i. symmetry. exact (Hfg i).
      + intros f g h Hfg Hgh i. transitivity (g i); [ exact (Hfg i) | exact (Hgh i) ].
    - intros f g Hfg i j Hij. rewrite Hij. exact (Hfg j).
    - intros f g Hfg i. exact (Hfg i).
    - intros f i. reflexivity.
    - intros t i. reflexivity.
  Qed.
End TableFun.
