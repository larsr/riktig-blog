The inner product of a sum with itself, expanded.
  Lemma inner_add_add : ∀ u v : V,
    ⟪ u + v , u + v ⟫ == (⟪ u , u ⟫ + ⟪ u , v ⟫) + (⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫).
  Proof.
    intros u v.
    calc (⟪ u + v , u + v ⟫)
      == (⟪ u , u + v ⟫ + ⟪ v , u + v ⟫) { (additive (f := fun w ⇒ ⟪ w , u + v ⟫) u v) }
      == (⟪ u + v , u ⟫ + ⟪ v , u + v ⟫) { (commutative (op := inner) u (u + v)) }
      == (⟪ u + v , u ⟫ + ⟪ u + v , v ⟫) { (commutative (op := inner) v (u + v)) }
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ + ⟪ v , u ⟫) + ⟪ u + v , v ⟫) { (additive (f := fun w ⇒ ⟪ w , u ⟫) u v) }
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ + ⟪ v , u ⟫) + (⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫)) { (additive (f := fun w ⇒ ⟪ w , v ⟫) u v) }
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ + ⟪ u , v ⟫) + (⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫)) { (commutative (op := inner) v u) }.
  Qed.

  Lemma inner_add_self : ∀ u v : V,
    ⟪ u + v , u + v ⟫ == ⟪ u , u ⟫ + ((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫).
  Proof.
    intros u v.
    calc (⟪ u + v , u + v ⟫)
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ + ⟪ u , v ⟫) + (⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫)) { inner_add_add }
      == (⟪ u , u ⟫ + (⟪ u , v ⟫ + (⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫))) { associative }
      == (⟪ u , u ⟫ + ((⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ u , v ⟫) + ⟪ v , v ⟫)) { associative }
      == (⟪ u , u ⟫ + ((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫)) { two_mul }.
  Qed.

The square of a sum of vectors, expanded.
  Lemma vec_add_sq : ∀ u v : V,
    vec (u + v) × vec (u + v) == vec u × vec u + ((vec u × vec v + vec v × vec u) + vec v × vec v).
  Proof.
    intros u v.
    calc (vec (u + v) × vec (u + v))
      == ((vec u + vec v) × (vec u + vec v)) { (additive (f := vec) u v) }
      == (vec u × (vec u + vec v) + vec v × (vec u + vec v)) { right_distributive }
      == ((vec u × vec u + vec u × vec v) + vec v × (vec u + vec v)) { left_distributive }
      == ((vec u × vec u + vec u × vec v) + (vec v × vec u + vec v × vec v)) { left_distributive }
      == (vec u × vec u + (vec u × vec v + (vec v × vec u + vec v × vec v))) { associative }
      == (vec u × vec u + ((vec u × vec v + vec v × vec u) + vec v × vec v)) { associative }.
  Qed.

  Lemma vec_add_sq_inner : ∀ u v : V,
    vec (u + v) × vec (u + v) == vec u × vec u + (((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: 1 + vec v × vec v).
  Proof.
    intros u v.
    calc (vec (u + v) × vec (u + v))
      == (⟪ u + v , u + v ⟫ *: (1 : A)) { vec_sq }
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ + ((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫)) *: (1 : A)) { inner_add_self }
      == (⟪ u , u ⟫ *: (1 : A) + ((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫ + ⟪ v , v ⟫) *: (1 : A)) { right_distributive }
      == (⟪ u , u ⟫ *: (1 : A) + (((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: (1 : A) + ⟪ v , v ⟫ *: (1 : A))) { right_distributive }
      == (vec u × vec u + (((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: (1 : A) + ⟪ v , v ⟫ *: (1 : A))) { vec_sq }
      == (vec u × vec u + (((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: (1 : A) + vec v × vec v)) { vec_sq }.
  Qed.

Polarisation: the symmetric part of a product of two vectors is twice their inner product.
  Theorem vec_mul_add_mul_rev : ∀ u v : V,
    vec u × vec v + vec v × vec u == ((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: 1.
  Proof.
    intros u v.
    apply (add_left_cancel (vec u × vec u)).
    apply (add_right_cancel (vec v × vec v)).
    calc ((vec u × vec u + (vec u × vec v + vec v × vec u)) + vec v × vec v)
      == (vec u × vec u + ((vec u × vec v + vec v × vec u) + vec v × vec v)) { associative }
      == (vec (u + v) × vec (u + v)) { vec_add_sq }
      == (vec u × vec u + (((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: (1 : A) + vec v × vec v)) { vec_add_sq_inner }
      == ((vec u × vec u + ((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: (1 : A)) + vec v × vec v) { associative }.
  Qed.

Orthogonal vectors anticommute.
  Theorem orthogonal_anticommute : ∀ u v : V,
    ⟪ u , v ⟫ == 0 → vec u × vec v == - (vec v × vec u).
  Proof.
    intros u v Huv.
    apply eq_neg_of_add_eq_zero_left.
    calc (vec u × vec v + vec v × vec u)
      == (((1 + 1) × ⟪ u , v ⟫) *: (1 : A)) { vec_mul_add_mul_rev }
      == (((1 + 1) × 0) *: (1 : A)) { Huv }
      == ((0 : F) *: (1 : A)) { (mul_zero (1 + 1 : F)) }
      == (0 : A) { (zero_smul (1 : A)) }.
  Qed.

  Lemma vec_mul_vec_mul_vec : ∀ u v : V,
    vec u × ((vec u × vec v) × vec v) == (⟪ u , u ⟫ × ⟪ v , v ⟫) *: 1.
  Proof.
    intros u v.
    calc (vec u × ((vec u × vec v) × vec v))
      == (vec u × (vec u × (vec v × vec v))) { associative }
      == ((vec u × vec u) × (vec v × vec v)) { associative }
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ *: (1 : A)) × (vec v × vec v)) { vec_sq }
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ *: (1 : A)) × (⟪ v , v ⟫ *: (1 : A))) { vec_sq }
      == ((⟪ u , u ⟫ × ⟪ v , v ⟫) *: (1 : A)) { smul_one_mul_smul_one }.
  Qed.

  Lemma orthogonal_mul_mul : ∀ u v : V, ⟪ v , u ⟫ == 0 →
    (vec u × vec v) × (vec u × vec v) == - (vec u × ((vec u × vec v) × vec v)).
  Proof.
    intros u v Hvu.
    calc ((vec u × vec v) × (vec u × vec v))
      == (vec u × (vec v × (vec u × vec v))) { (associative (op := mult) (action := mult) (vec u) (vec v) (vec u × vec v)) }
      == (vec u × ((vec v × vec u) × vec v)) { (associative (op := mult) (action := mult) (vec v) (vec u) (vec v)) }
      == (vec u × (- (vec u × vec v) × vec v)) { (orthogonal_anticommute v u Hvu) }
      == (vec u × - ((vec u × vec v) × vec v)) { (neg_mul (vec u × vec v) (vec v)) }
      == (- (vec u × ((vec u × vec v) × vec v))) { (mul_neg (vec u) ((vec u × vec v) × vec v)) }.
  Qed.

The product of two orthogonal vectors squares to minus the product of their squared lengths: for unit vectors, to - 1.
  Theorem orthogonal_mul_sq : ∀ u v : V, ⟪ u , v ⟫ == 0 →
    (vec u × vec v) × (vec u × vec v) == - (⟪ u , u ⟫ × ⟪ v , v ⟫) *: 1.
  Proof.
    intros u v Huv.
    assert (Hvu : ⟪ v , u ⟫ == 0) by (setoid_rewrite (commutative (op := inner) v u); exact Huv).
    calc ((vec u × vec v) × (vec u × vec v))
      == (- (vec u × ((vec u × vec v) × vec v))) { (orthogonal_mul_mul u v Hvu) }
      == (- ((⟪ u , u ⟫ × ⟪ v , v ⟫) *: (1 : A))) { vec_mul_vec_mul_vec }
      == (- (⟪ u , u ⟫ × ⟪ v , v ⟫) *: (1 : A)) { (neg_smul (⟪ u , u ⟫ × ⟪ v , v ⟫) (1 : A)) }.
  Qed.

A vector of nonzero squared length is invertible: its inverse is itself, scaled by the inverse of its squared length.
  Theorem vec_mul_inv : ∀ u : V, ⟪ u , u ⟫ =/= 0 →
    vec u × (inv ⟪ u , u ⟫ *: vec u) == 1.
  Proof.
    intros u Hu.
    calc (vec u × (inv ⟪ u , u ⟫ *: vec u))
      == (inv ⟪ u , u ⟫ *: (vec u × vec u)) { (mul_smul_comm (inv ⟪ u , u ⟫) (vec u) (vec u)) }
      == (inv ⟪ u , u ⟫ *: (⟪ u , u ⟫ *: (1 : A))) { vec_sq }
      == ((inv ⟪ u , u ⟫ × ⟪ u , u ⟫) *: (1 : A)) { (associative (op := mult) (action := scale) (inv ⟪ u , u ⟫) ⟪ u , u ⟫ (1 : A)) }
      == ((1 : F) *: (1 : A)) { (left_inverse_on (defined_on := nonzero F) ⟪ u , u ⟫ Hu) }
      == (1 : A) { (left_identity (action := scale) (1 : A)) }.
  Qed.
