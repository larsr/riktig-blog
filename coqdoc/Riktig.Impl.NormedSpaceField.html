An ordered field is a normed space over itself: the norm is the absolute value, and the norm laws are the absolute value's.
Section FieldNorm.
  Context {F : Type} `{OrderedField F}.

  #[export] Instance Field_Norm : HasNorm F F := abs.

  #[export] Instance Field_NormedSpace : NormedSpace F F Field_Norm :=

       NormedSpace.eq_zero_of_norm_eq_zero := eq_zero_of_abs_eq_zero;
       NormedSpace.norm_smul := abs_mul;
       NormedSpace.norm_add_le := abs_triangle |}.
End FieldNorm.
