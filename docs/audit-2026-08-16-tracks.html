Audit 2026-08-16 — the self-contained tracks

Scope: theories/Programs/**, theories/Channel/**, theories/Impl/Lambda/**, theories/Examples/**, theories/Lib/**, and the track-facing theory files theories/Theory/{Merge,MergeSort,Quicksort,BinarySearch,Sorted,Subseq,Gcd,Parser,DelayedFusion}.v plus theories/Theory/Memprog/*.v — about 14 400 lines, judged against both docs/DESIGN.md’s Program-style rules 1–6 and its structure rules. The tree compiles clean at this commit (make exit 0, 210 .vo), and every axiom claim below was checked with Print Assumptions against that build rather than inferred from the source. 49 findings: 6 high, 16 medium, 27 low. The one soundness-relevant finding is an undisclosed stdlib axiom under Programs/Quicksort.v that every theorem in Theory/Quicksort.v inherits. The other five high findings are structural: four large duplications (the Church theorems, the two ARQ protocols, the two ABNF grammars, and — at medium — the planar/spatial robot arm), and a datatype-plus-algorithm file filed in Theory/. The long tail of low findings is dominated by one pattern: Lib/ is not used. Lib/ListFacts.v is imported by exactly one file in the whole tree while six files in scope keep private copies of general list facts.

What is clean

Most of what was checked is in good order, and the tracks are in better shape than “less attention than the algebra tower” would predict. Programs/Merge.v is a genuine exemplar and Programs/Gcd.v follows it exactly, abstract lia and all; Programs/Memprog/Quicksort.v’s four builders reproduce the model’s shape at a much harder termination argument, with the guard-as-match-on-dec reason written down. Theory/Gcd.v is the best small file in scope — one equivalence to Nat.gcd, then everything through it, which is what rule 5’s “specs owe an equivalence proof to a canonical formulation” asks for. The Memprog semantics layer is the rule-3 exemplar the tracks needed: interp_mono, interp_sound, interp_adequate, eval_interp, with both Semantics.v and Equiv.v opening by telling the reader not to invert Eval and giving the Type-index reason. Theory/Lambda.v derives ten results from the LambdaModel equations with no induction over syntax at all, in calc chains that stay under seven steps and carry the carrier ascriptions rule 10 prescribes. Programs/RobotArm/RobotArm.v names every clause equation and cites the ring laws by their bare general projections. Theory/Sorted.v, Theory/Subseq.v, Theory/Merge.v and Theory/BinarySearch.v are small, general and correctly layered, and Theory/Memprog/View.v’s mem_read_cells_0/_S are introduced with the explicit note “so no proof below unfolds the fixpoint”. The disclosure culture is strong throughout: Channel/ABP.v:239 ships the counterexample showing why subseq cannot be strengthened to prefix, Impl/Lambda/SKI.v:276 states that no simulation theorem exists for translate and computes the two normal forms that refute it, Channel/GoBackN.v:1 says what never-wrapping sequence numbers do not model, Channel/Ack.v:1 flags that its per-round model cannot pose the cross-round reordering question, and Theory/DelayedFusion.v:306 prints its own assumptions. Outside Programs/Quicksort.v the tracks are axiom-free — msort_correct, bsearch_sound, abp_ack_prefix, gbn_prefix and FST_PAIR all report “closed under the global context” — and there is no Axiom, no Admitted and no admit anywhere in scope.
