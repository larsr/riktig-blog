Audit 2026-08-16 — the linear-algebra and geometric-algebra theories

Area: theories/Theory/{Basis, MatRep, MatVec, MatMulBilinear, Determinant, Trace, SubspaceBasis, Subspace, GramSchmidt, KerIm, Biproduct, DualSpace, DualBasis, Transport, VSIso, Hom, HomVS, Bilinear, BilinearVS, LinearMap, Exterior, Jacobian, Backprop, BigOp, Fin, Graded, Circular, GeometricAlgebra, GAWedgeDot, InnerGeometry, SpaceRotor, PlaneRotor, SU3Algebra}.v, ~11 000 lines. Every finding below was read, not merely grepped; the greps only produced the candidate list.

41 findings: 12 high, 22 medium, 7 low. They concentrate in three places. The rotor files (Theory/SpaceRotor.v, Theory/PlaneRotor.v, and the last third of Theory/Circular.v) are Impl code filed as Theory: they are stated over Cl F n = Blade n -> F, they define thirteen computing functions, and seventeen of their lemmas are closed by one unfold …; simpl; ring tactic that opens the Clifford product down to its bit-vector representation. The determinant chain carries two Context (detM_mul : …) sections and a Q-instantiated corollary that Impl/DetMulGeneral.v made unnecessary when it proved detM_mul for every Field. And Theory/Subspace.v never names the one equation its carrier is defined by, so 29 proof steps across three files re-establish it with change (proj1_sig …). The rest of the area — Basis, KerIm, Biproduct, DualSpace, Transport, GramSchmidt, Graded, InnerGeometry, GeometricAlgebra, the abstract two thirds of Circular, and BigOp — is in good order.

Item numbers below are the five checklist items; #103 refers to the deferred Fin.t/Matrix inventory in docs/TODO.md, which is not re-litigated here.

What is clean

Theory/Basis.v, Theory/KerIm.v, Theory/Biproduct.v, Theory/DualSpace.v, Theory/Transport.v, Theory/Graded.v, Theory/GeometricAlgebra.v, Theory/InnerGeometry.v and the first two thirds of Theory/Circular.v are what the rules describe: statements over class parameters, proofs that cite the general projections, and no concrete type in sight. Theory/GramSchmidt.v is the model for how a recursive algorithm should reach Theory — five equations as a class, the recursion in Impl/GramSchmidt.v, and every property derived from the equations alone; its inductions are on list, which rule 16 makes ambient. Theory/Fin.v names no Fin.t, as #101 claimed and as a grep confirms. Theory/BigOp.v’s 54 case analyses are all on nat and list, not on an index representation. Theory/GAWedgeDot.v is, apart from the two hand-derivations above, an exemplary piece of work: it states the wedge-dot presentation as a class and proves the equivalence with GeometricAlgebra in both directions, with Print Assumptions on all three main results. And Theory/Biproduct.v is the clearest statement in the area of what this audit’s high findings are asking for: the same theorems the matrix files prove entrywise, proved index-free from the universal properties, in a third of the lines.
