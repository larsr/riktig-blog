Finding: lincomb at a concrete carrier resolves to the multiplicative monoid

Severity: high. Found 2026-08-16 while writing Examples/GettingStarted.v for the getting-started post; recorded here with the sweep’s other findings.

What happens

Structure/Basis.v binds {HasLincomb I F V}, and Impl/FinIndex.v supplies HasLincomb_enum, whose sum is a bigopI over a Monoid. At V := Fin.t n -> Q, instance search fills that Monoid argument with the pointwise multiplicative monoid (@PW_Monoid (t 4) Q Q_EqRel Q_One Q_Mult Q_MulMonoid) rather than the additive one. Two consequences, both silent:

  • Definition B4 : Basis 4 (Fin.t 4) Q (Fin.t 4 -> Q) := FinVec_Basis 4. does not typecheck, though the term is the intended basis; the workaround is to let the type be read off the term (Definition B4 := FinVec_Basis (F := Q) 4.).
  • Worse, a statement written by hand elaborates against the wrong monoid and then computes the wrong number: reconstruction at u4 reduced to 0 at index 1 (a product one of whose factors is zero) where the intended sum is 2.

Nothing fails loudly: the build is green, the wrong reading is a well-typed statement about a different monoid.

Why it matters

Every Basis statement instantiated at a concrete carrier is exposed to this. The abstract theory is unaffected — over an anonymous carrier the binder is whatever the caller supplied — so the defect appears exactly where a reader is most likely to be: writing their first concrete example.

Fix, one sentence

Give HasLincomb_enum (and the bigopI layer beneath it) a Hint Mode or a pinned additive-monoid argument so the additive reading is the only one search can produce at a carrier with both.

Fixed

HasLincomb_enum no longer takes a Monoid V. The fold it defines uses Zero V and HasAdd V and nothing else — the same division Impl/FinIndex.v already states for bigop, where the operation classes carry the fold and the law instances carry the monoid — and those two goals have one solution each once the carrier is known, so the additive reading is the only one search can produce. The Monoid stays on Lincomb_enum, whose class does take the operation as a parameter, so a reader sees which monoid an equation is about.

Tightening Monoid’s own Hint Mode to ! - ! ! was tried first and rejected: Equivalence (@equ M _) is reached through Monoid -> Semigroup -> Magma, so the flexible mode is what lets a calc chain in a monoid section find its own transitivity, and the tree stops building at Theory/Monoid.v:10.

Both consequences are regression Examples in theories/Examples/GettingStarted.v: B4 is ascribed Basis 4 (Fin.t 4) Q (Fin.t 4 -> Q) again, and reconstruct_u4_at_1 computes the reconstruction at index 1 and asks for 2, which the multiplicative reading answers with 0.
