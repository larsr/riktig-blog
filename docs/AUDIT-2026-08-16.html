The checklist

  1. A specification names an implementation type (rule 16).
  2. A general law re-derived by hand (rule 8) — fingerprint: a calc step justified by right_inverse/left_inverse mid-chain, which is neg_unique_l unfolded.
  3. A special case proved where the general theorem exists or should.
  4. A proof welded to a representation.
  5. A missing corollary that makes callers repeat bookkeeping.

What to decide first

Ranked by what they cost if left, not by how many lines they touch.

  1. An undeclared axiom in a correctness proof. Programs/Quicksort.v:59 closes Fix_eq’s premise with functional_extensionality, which DESIGN rule 6 forbids by name, and line 20 Require Exports the axiom module, so it reaches every importer. Print Assumptions qsort_correct reports functional_extensionality_dep (verified). The file carries no header disclosure, so docs/TODO.md’s inventory — which names Programs/ — is right by accident and docs/DESIGN.md rule 5’s “disclosed in the header of the file that uses it” is unmet. msort_correct and bsearch_sound are clean. Eliminated 2026-08-16: part now packs (m, (store, i)) into its Fix argument, so the congruence premise is an equation between mem * nat values; the require is gone and all three of qsort_correct, msort_correct, bsearch_sound are closed under the global context.
  2. lincomb at a concrete carrier resolves to the multiplicative monoid, silently: a hand-written reconstruction computes 0 where 2 is meant, and the build stays green (detail). FIXED: HasLincomb_enum asks for Zero V and HasAdd V and no longer for a Monoid V, which is what left the operation free; the two consequences are regression Examples in theories/Examples/GettingStarted.v.
  3. Two Theory files are implementations. Not because they compute — computation in a proof is legitimate once the laws are the specification and the carrier is proved to satisfy them; the bridge is what makes the computation mean something. The defect is the direction: Theory/Maxwell.v’s theorems are STATEMENTS about Cl F 4, so the definition specifies and nothing transports. Its fix is to state the results over the geometric-algebra structure and let by_blade discharge them through the Cl_GA instance that already exists — keep the tactic, change the claim. Theory/DelayedFusion.v is a program filed as a theory; Theory/KleisliIso.v turns concrete past line 80.
  4. The rotor files cannot see their own GA instance — same direction problem, same remedy: restate over the structure, keep the blade computation as the discharge. SpaceRotor and PlaneRotor are stated over Cl F n directly, so contraction and polarization never match; seventeen lemmas are closed by unfolding to blades. rotor_mul is now proved abstractly in Theory/Circular.v; sandwich_compose is done by blade computation while the identical rotate_compose one file later is done from associative.
  5. The determinant chain assumes a hypothesis the tree discharged. Theory/Determinant.v and Theory/Exterior.v take detM_mul as a section hypothesis, each with a comment explaining it is only available downstream; Impl/DetMulGeneral.v proves it for every field, and nothing requires that file. FIXED: Theory/Determinant.v requires it and Theory/Exterior.v reaches it from there, so det_comp, det_basis_independent and omega_B_lm hold over any field and the Q corollaries are gone. The require is a Theory/ -> Impl/ edge and is documented as such in the header, beside the Impl/Det.v one it already had.
  6. bsum is bigopI written twice — ten laws re-proved in Impl/Clifford.v that Theory/BigOp.v already has for any FinIndex carrier; task #113 dissolves it.
  7. The Lambda track ignores its own specification. Church.v re-proves by hand on tm the four results Theory/Lambda.v derives from the laws; Scoped.v and SKI.v are two further representations with no instance.
  8. Duplicated developments: Channel/ABP.v and GoBackN.v differ only in the tag type (which GoBackN.v:3 names and declines to abstract); the ABNF grammar is written twice; SubspaceBasis.v holds three hand-written copies of one construction.

Negative results, recorded on purpose

  • The rule-8 fingerprint does not occur in Programs, Channel, Lambda, Examples or Lib: those tracks contain almost no group algebra, and the two files that touch the tower cite the general projections correctly.
  • SU3Algebra.v is acquitted of the GA charge — no Clifford product, no quadratic form, so contraction and polarization have nothing to say there. Its gap is that the tree has no Structure/LieAlgebra.v.
  • No Axiom, Admitted or admit anywhere in the tracks; the one axiom is the undisclosed import above.
