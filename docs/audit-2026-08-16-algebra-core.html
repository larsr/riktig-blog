32 findings: 4 high, 21 medium, 7 low. The high ones are all one shape — two Theory files (Maxwell.v, KleisliIso.v) import Impl and state their theorems about the concrete carrier that lives there, which rule 1 names as its most common violation. The medium band splits three ways: eight lemmas stated at one carrier or one filter whose proofs use nothing specific to it (the is_deriv_*_equ family, the Limits/Continuity twins), four missing corollaries that make every epsilon argument in Limits/Continuity/Derivative repeat the same six lines of bookkeeping, and four proofs that reach through a product or option representation instead of an equation. Two claims below were checked by compiling them against the built library (lt_of_not_le, is_deriv_L_equ at U -> V, Group.monoid := Prod_Monoid, add_sub_cancel_r); the rest are read, not run.

What is clean

Structure/ came through the whole checklist without a finding: all 37 files were read, and none names a concrete or Impl-owned type — the index carrier is FinIndex n I rather than Fin.t (Structure/Fin.v, Structure/Basis.v, both of which explain the choice in a comment), the arity of a multilinear form is an instantiation rather than a parameter (Structure/Bilinear.v:137-142), and Structure/Series.v and Structure/Graded.v use nat only as a sequence or grade index. The law-class assembly is uniform: Magma through Field restate nothing their substructure field implies, and the Hint Modes put ! on the carrier alone. Theory/Group.v and Theory/Monoid.v are what the rest should look like — no inline re-derivations, every step a named law, and the AdditiveTheory section at Group.v:142-168 is exactly the general form (additive_zero, additive_negation) that Theory/Ring.v, Theory/VectorSpace.v and Theory/InnerProductSpace.v then cite instead of reproving; that mechanism is working. The Theory/AbGroup.v cancellation family (sub_pair, sub_mid_cancel, four_term_cancel and the rest) looked at first like lemma sprawl, but every one has a real call site in Derivative.v, BilinearDeriv.v, Norm.v or Circular.v, which is rule 4 working as intended, not against it. Theory/Field.v’s mul_commutative is a legitimate disambiguation, not a restatement, since commutative is ambiguous on a field’s two operations. Nothing in the area contains an Axiom, an Admitted, or an undisclosed classical import.
